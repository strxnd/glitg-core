# Server-rule ownership

The bundled policy enforces the deterministic server-side rules: Diamond-tier armour/weapons, Netherite restrictions, enchantment caps/bans, prohibited items, grouped carried-item limits, potion effects and durations, one normal Mace craft, Mace/TNT-minecart damage caps, global grace, combat/danger logging, post-death protection, combat-only equipment and material restrictions, bed/anchor bombing bans, End locking, the anonymous-death timer, automatic villager restocks, and doubled Breeze Rod drops.

`/start` begins the one-hour global grace period, schedules the End to open after 604800 seconds, and starts invisible-player death-message hiding for the same duration.

GLITG Core does **not** implement a season timeline or legendary-item framework. The external heart plugin owns heart transfer, weekly minimums, crafted/withdrawn hearts, heart equipment, revives, and final-day death bans. The second Dragon-Egg Mace and lost-item recreation remain staff/external responsibilities.

Anti-cheat, client-mod policy, claims/rollback, public-base review, offline-raiding adjudication, team tier classifications, and farm/lag reasonableness remain external systems or staff policy.
